Why this works

Strong
  • The map is the product, not a decoration of it.
  • Heat on the map gives live intelligence, not empty geography.
  • The ticker solves the dead-product problem by proving activity.
  • Emotionally it says this is about the whole country, not one city.

Where it can fail

Watch these
  • Empty map is worse than no map.
  • Ticker without real data becomes noise immediately.
  • Mobile cannot carry a 543-cell map as the hero.
  • Citizen action can disappear if the observer layer dominates too hard.

The dependency

Critical
  • You need the real 543-constituency TopoJSON.
  • Seed 50–100 constituencies with real or highly realistic issue data.
  • Ticker speed should complete one full cycle in roughly 18–20 seconds.

Verdict: Do it — but solve the two-audience problem first

The map-as-landing is the right direction for this moment. It is visually powerful, intellectually honest to the mission, and immediately communicates scale in a way no headline can.

The decision is strategic: if the page is observer-first, let the map dominate. If it is citizen-first, keep `Submit Issue` dominant and let the map support.
